A sacred course in five parts, ninety-nine meetings.

A Sacred Educational Course

WHAT IS. FOUNDATION?

A 99-meeting course built on a parable of light, the prism, the tablets, and the rainbow staircase. God is light. The prism guides our travel. The tablets carry the ten words. The rainbow is a staircase of light. Guided forward, we become light.

Session Zero

introduction and overview and purpose

Meeting 0

Part 1

God is Light

Meetings 1–7

Part one is the most foundational and the heaviest. It is saturated in the identity of God as light and as love. Yeshuah of Nazareth is the light of the world, the meeting facilitator, the soul of creation. Light leads creation into a face-to-face encounter with God.

Meeting 1

God is light 1 — what is light? Light is love

Brief

Light is the love leader. God is light. God is love. Light is love.

Explore the sun as the metaphor of God's movement toward creation. Yeshuah is the light of the world, the great meeting facilitator, descending into darkness to lead us into union with God.

Open meeting

Meeting 2

God is light 2 — what is love? Mapping love

Brief

Love is not a state. Love is an ordered activity.

Build a map of love: the lover seeks, joins, and attaches. The loved receives and responds in kind. The result is a new shape — the two becoming one, sharing being.

Open meeting

Meeting 3

God is light 3 — THE 3 WITHS OF LOVE / LOVE AND CREATION

Brief

The three withs of creation: God alone, God with creation, God within creation.

GOD WANTS TO SEEK YOU — the Father, glory beyond. GOD WANTS TO JOIN WITH YOU — the Son, the word and soul of creation. GOD WANTS TO ATTACH WITHIN YOU — the Spirit, glory within the created microcosm.

Open meeting

Meeting 4

God is light 4 — God loves first. Yahweh formula. the shape of love

Brief

The Yahweh formula: God revealing God. God is always the lover who initiates.

Study the shapes God takes to do the activity of love. The face of God revealing himself. The pre-shaping required for each step of the lover's movement.

Open meeting

Meeting 5

God is light 5 — BELOVED. Love's Reflection: the Image of love

Brief

Who is God approaching when he approaches us? You are beloved.

You are God's beloved child. You are pleasing to God. You are chosen by God. This is our true identity before him — the word of the gospel.

Open meeting

Meeting 6

God is light 6 — the shema / loving love back

Brief

The Shema is the response of the loved to the lover.

Being the responder to God the lover when we are the loved. The shema as the form of loving love back.

Open meeting

Meeting 7

God is light 7 — give my flesh and blood / neighbor love

Brief

Giving my flesh and blood as a meal to my neighbor.

Being the responder to God the lover when we are the loved through creation — neighbor love, love neighbor as yourself. The final movement of God is light, bridging into the prism.

Part 4

The Rainbow

Meetings 23–29

Part four reveals the rainbow as a staircase of light. The seven spaces descend from violet to red, mapping conscience, mind, heart, gut, members, people, and earth onto their corresponding spaces in the temple.

Meeting 23

The Rainbow — Space 7 — Violet | Conscience | Ark

Brief

The highest space: conscience, mapped to the Ark.

Violet light. The innermost dimension of the human being — conscience — corresponds to the Ark of the Covenant, the dwelling place of God.

Open meeting

Meeting 24

The Rainbow — Space 6 — Indigo | Mind | Holy of Holies

Brief

Space 6: mind, mapped to the Holy of Holies.

Indigo light. The mind as the gate of the innermost sanctuary. The Holy of Holies is the space where the mind meets the divine.

Open meeting

Meeting 25

The Rainbow — Space 5 — Blue | Heart | Holy Place

Brief

Space 5: heart, mapped to the Holy Place.

Blue light. The heart as the meeting place of God and humanity. The Holy Place holds the lampstand, the bread, and the altar of incense.

Open meeting

Meeting 26

The Rainbow — Space 4 — Green | Gut | Courtyard

Brief

Space 4: gut, mapped to the Courtyard.

Green light. The gut as the seat of instinct and vitality, corresponding to the outer courtyard where offering and washing occur.

Open meeting

Meeting 27

The Rainbow — Space 3 — Yellow | Members | Levitical Barrier

Brief

Space 3: members, mapped to the Levitical Barrier.

Yellow light. The members — hands, feet, voice — as the active expression of the inner life, bounded by the Levitical zone of service.

Open meeting

Meeting 28

The Rainbow — Space 2 — Orange | People | Tents of Israel

Brief

Space 2: people, mapped to the Tents of Israel.

Orange light. The relational and communal dimension, corresponding to the encampment of the tribes around the tabernacle.

Open meeting

Meeting 29

The Rainbow — Space 1 — Red | Earth | The Land of Israel

Brief

Space 1: earth, mapped to the Land of Israel.

Red light. The outermost space — the physical world, the land, the ground of all being — is the foundation from which we ascend.

Open meeting
