Sacred learning path

whatis.foundation

A sacred educational course with five transformational parts across 99 meetings. Each part offers a guided container for inner practice, shared learning, and the unfolding of a deeper invitation.

Course structure

99 meetings in five parts

This journey is divided into five parts, each with a specific focus and rhythm. Together they form an integrated curriculum for embodiment, inquiry, devotion, and community.

Part 1

Foundations of Presence

19 meetings

Begin the course by establishing presence, resonance, and the first practices of attention.

Part 2

Healing the Field

20 meetings

Deepen inner healing, relational awareness, and the alchemy of heart-based practice.

Part 3

Embodied Wisdom

20 meetings

Enter the embodied thread of wisdom through practice, dialogue, and creative exploration.

Part 4

Initiation of Knowing

20 meetings

Open into deep inquiry, initiation, and the unfolding of a more intimate relationship with truth.

Part 5

Integration & Service

20 meetings

Bring the course into expression, service, and a grounded commitment to ongoing practice.
