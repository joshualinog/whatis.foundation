    Teacher notes

    The three "holies" point to the three withs of GOD, being (without, with, within) like the prophet, this is our introduction to God.

    There is also a peculiar connect to the centrality of the concept term GLORY. Each existential, ontological space in the withs has its "glory". It is essentially the "soul" of that cosm/space. The glory of an individual creation within the set of creation is the soul of that individual, and The spirit of GOD is GOD being the "soul" of that soul at the level of "WITHIN". That is the ultimate location in the inner experience of that individual creation. The creation also has an outer experience. This would be all other creations alongside this individual creation (including even the entire set of creation itself). The soul/apex of the entire apex of the set of creation enthrones The glory of creation, the glory that is GOD WITH US, God with creation in its outer / other / separate experiences. There is also a space/cosm that is BEYOND creation, or WITHOUT creation. This is the father. The son is the glory of WITH creation. The Father is the glory of the glory of creation, and is himself, only self and himself "outside of" / beyond / without creation. In this sense, created things / creations have all three ontological conceptual cosms. But GOD alone has no "WITHOUT" or "BEYOND" ontological cosm
