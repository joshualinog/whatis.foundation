  • SHAPING - BEING IS CHOOSING A SHAPE, TO EN-SHAPE, TO POLY SHAPE
    1.1 concept_term: SHAPING / to shape
    1.2 concept_term: SPACE /
    1.3 • the beyond space is occupied, therefore there is life; consequently there is life and the ability / context to shape
    1.4 inner shaping
    1.5 outer shaping
    1.6. why shape? to combine, to join, to share being, and to co-shape into a new joined being. With this anticipation all shaping is PRE-shaping. <concept_term>
